# Product: Lazarus

Lazarus turns an old Android phone into a free AI server that answers people by plain SMS.

## Users
- People with button phones or no mobile data: farmers, elderly parents, drivers, daily-wage workers.
- Family members who set up the gateway phone for them.
- Hackathon judges, who test through the web Ouija simulator.

## Promise
- Text a question, get a short, useful answer by SMS.
- Works in English, Urdu script and Roman Urdu. Reply in the language of the question.
- No app, internet or account needed on the sender's side.

## Commands
HELP, STOP, SCAM <text>, REMIND <time> <text>, TRANSLATE <text>, or any free question.

## Safety rules (never break)
- STOP always wins: after STOP, never send to that number again until it texts START.
- Health, legal and money answers stay general and suggest a professional.
- Never invent prices or facts; say when unsure.
- Rate limit each number; allowlist mode available.
- Never log full phone numbers in the dashboard; mask all but last 3 digits.

## Tone
Short, kind, plain words. A reply should fit in 1 to 3 SMS parts.
