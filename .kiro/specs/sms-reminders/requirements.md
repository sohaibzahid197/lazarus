# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature makes the REMIND command work. Today the Gateway_Pipeline answers `REMIND <time> <text>` with the Not_Available_Text (necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 6 criterion 6). After this feature, a Sender can text `REMIND 8pm take medicine`, `REMIND 20:00 call Ali`, `YAAD kal subah 9 dawai` or `REMIND in 2 hours drink water` and get one SMS back at that time. Times are read in Pakistan_Time. A Sender can also text LIST to see their Reminders and CANCEL n to remove one.

The work is split the same way as the existing gateway:

- `packages/brain` gets the pure Reminder_Module: the Time_Parser, the Time_Printer, the due-time resolver, the reminder decisions inside the Gateway_Pipeline, the Delivery_Decision, the List_Formatter and the Reminder_Texts. The Reminder_Module has no React Native, Android, storage, network or system-clock dependency. Every current time is passed in as milliseconds since the Unix epoch.
- `apps/necromancer` stores Reminders in the existing `lazarus.db` SQLite database, sets Android alarms with the Reminder_Scheduler, and carries out the Delivery_Decision through the existing Sms_Sender. The Necromancer_App contains no time parsing or reminder decision logic of its own.

This document extends the necromancer-sms-gateway and brain-command-router specs. Terms defined in those specs (Gateway_Pipeline, Gateway_State, Canonical_Number, Stop_List, Allowlist_Mode, Rate_Limiter, Daily_Send_Cap, Outcome, Activity_Entry, Masked_Number, Reply_Text, Reply_Parts, SMS_Splitter, Command_Router, Language, Usage_Hint_Text, Try_Later_Text, Pending_Stop_Record, Paused_Path_Task, Gateway_Service, Boot_Receiver, Sms_Sender, Send_Result, Send_Timeout, Native_Module, Main_Screen, Settings_Screen, Brain_Test_Suite, App_Test_Suite, Inbound_Message, Automated_Sender, Own_Number, Allowlist, Number_Masker, Activity_Log, Help_Text, Not_Available_Text, Gemini_Api_Key, Answer_Service, Keyword, Alias, Alias_Table, Argument, Normalized_Message, Language_Detector, Command_Printer, Routed_Command) keep their meaning and are repeated in the Glossary only where this feature changes them.

### Decisions made in this document

Decisions marked "Confirmed by the user" were accepted at requirements review.

- **LIST and CANCEL are new top-level Commands with a strict form.** A message routes to LIST only when the keyword stands alone, and to CANCEL only when the keyword stands alone or is followed by one whole number. Any other message starting with `list` or `cancel` (for example `list of foods for sugar patients`) stays a QUESTION, so free questions are not taken over.
- **LIST and CANCEL keywords are fixed.** LIST is `list`, `fehrist` or `فہرست`; CANCEL is `cancel`, `mansookh` or `منسوخ`. Lowercase `list` and `cancel` are accepted through the case-insensitive matching of Requirement 1.1. Confirmed by the user.
- **A bare hour with no Day_Period and no `am` or `pm` is rejected.** `REMIND 9 take medicine` gets the Time_Help_Text with example REMIND messages instead of a guessed time (Requirement 2.12). Confirmed by the user.
- **A Day_Period with no Clock_Time uses a default hour.** Morning (`subah`) is 09:00, evening (`shaam`) is 18:00 and night (`raat`) is 21:00 (Requirement 2.8). Confirmed by the user.
- **Reminders use stable slot numbers.** Each active Reminder of a Canonical_Number has a Reminder_Slot from 1 to 5. CANCEL n removes the Reminder in slot n, so the number shown in a confirmation or a LIST reply stays valid even after another Reminder is delivered.
- **A Reminder is only ever sent to the number that created it.** There is no way to set a Reminder for another phone number.
- **The confirmation SMS shows the slot and the Due_Time, not the Reminder_Text**, so the confirmation always fits 1 SMS part in every Language.
- **Reminder deliveries count toward the Daily_Send_Cap but not toward the Rate_Limiter.** The Rate_Limiter counts Inbound_Messages; a delivery is not an Inbound_Message. Confirmed by the user.
- **Late Reminders are dropped after the Late_Window of 60 minutes.** If the phone was off, the gateway was paused or Android delayed the alarm for longer than 60 minutes past the Due_Time, the Reminder is not sent. Confirmed by the user.
- **The Reminder_Text is the only part of a body text that is persisted** outside the existing Pending_Inbox exception. The Reminder_Text is stored in the `lazarus.db` SQLite database and deleted when the Reminder is delivered, cancelled, expired or removed by STOP. Confirmed by the user.
- **No calendar dates.** The Time_Parser reads clock times, today, tomorrow, parts of the day and relative durations only; dates such as `15 Nov` are not supported. Confirmed by the user.

### Known limitations

- **Force stop removes alarms.** When the Operator force-stops the Necromancer_App from Android settings, Android cancels its alarms until the app is opened again. Reminders that become due before the next start are delivered under the Late_Window rule or expire.
- **Inexact alarms can be late.** WHILE Exact_Alarm_Access is not granted, Android can deliver an alarm many minutes after the Due_Time, especially while the phone is idle.
- **Fixed time zone.** Pakistan_Time is UTC+05:00 with no daylight saving time. A Sender in another time zone gets Reminders in Pakistan_Time.
- **No calendar dates.** The Time_Parser reads clock times, today, tomorrow, parts of the day and relative durations. Dates such as `15 Nov` are not supported; `in 10 days` covers dates up to 30 days ahead.

## Glossary

- **Reminder_Module**: The pure TypeScript reminder logic in `packages/brain`, exported from `@lazarus/brain`, made of the Time_Parser, the Time_Printer, the Due_Time_Resolver, the Reminder_Store_Model, the Delivery_Decision, the List_Formatter and the Reminder_Texts.
- **Reminder**: One stored request to send one SMS to one Canonical_Number at one Due_Time, made of a Reminder_Id, the Canonical_Number, the Reminder_Slot, the Due_Time, the Reminder_Text, the Language of the REMIND message and the creation time.
- **Reminder_Id**: A unique identifier of a Reminder within the Reminder_Store.
- **Reminder_Slot**: An integer from 1 to 5 that identifies an active Reminder among the active Reminders of one Canonical_Number. No two active Reminders of the same Canonical_Number share a Reminder_Slot.
- **Active_Reminder**: A Reminder in the Reminder_Store that has not been delivered, cancelled, expired or removed by STOP.
- **Reminder_Limit**: The maximum of 5 Active_Reminders per Canonical_Number.
- **Reminder_Horizon**: The maximum time of 30 days (2,592,000,000 milliseconds) between the receive time of a REMIND message and the Due_Time of the Reminder it creates.
- **Late_Window**: The maximum time of 60 minutes after the Due_Time during which a Reminder can still be delivered.
- **Due_Time**: The moment, in milliseconds since the Unix epoch, at which a Reminder is to be delivered.
- **Pakistan_Time**: The time zone Asia/Karachi, treated as a fixed offset of UTC+05:00 with no daylight saving time.
- **Reminder_Store**: The persisted set of Reminders, kept in a table of the existing `lazarus.db` SQLite database of the Necromancer_App.
- **Reminder_Store_Model**: The pure functions in the Reminder_Module that add, remove and look up Reminders in an in-memory copy of the Reminder_Store and produce the matching state changes for the Necromancer_App to persist.
- **Time_Expression**: The structured form of the time part of a REMIND argument. One of: a Clock_Time with an optional Day_Word and an optional Day_Period; a Day_Period alone with an optional Day_Word; or a Relative_Duration.
- **Clock_Time**: An hour and minute written as a 12-hour time with `am` or `pm` (for example `8pm`, `8:30 pm`, `8.30pm`), as a 24-hour time with a colon (for example `20:00`, `9:05`), or as a bare hour of 1 to 12 that follows a Day_Period (for example `subah 9`).
- **Day_Word**: A word that names the day: `today`, `aaj` or `آج` for the current Pakistan_Time day, and `tomorrow`, `kal` or `کل` for the next Pakistan_Time day.
- **Day_Period**: A word that names a part of the day: morning (`morning`, `subah`, `صبح`), evening (`evening`, `shaam`, `شام`) or night (`night`, `raat`, `رات`).
- **Relative_Duration**: A whole number followed by a unit, in one of the forms `in N minutes`, `in N hours`, `in N days` (with the singular and short forms `minute`, `min`, `mins`, `hour`, `hr`, `hrs`, `day`), `N minute baad`, `N ghante baad`, `N ghanta baad`, `N din baad`, `N منٹ بعد`, `N گھنٹے بعد`, `N گھنٹہ بعد` or `N دن بعد`.
- **Filler_Word**: A word that the Time_Parser skips inside or right after a Time_Expression: `at`, `ko`, `baje`, `کو`, `بجے`.
- **Time_Parser**: The pure function in the Reminder_Module that reads the leading Time_Expression of a REMIND argument and returns either the Time_Expression together with the remaining Reminder_Text, or a Parse_Error.
- **Parse_Error**: The result of the Time_Parser or the Due_Time_Resolver when no Reminder can be created. One of `no-time` (no Time_Expression at the start of the argument), `no-text` (a Time_Expression with no Reminder_Text after it), `text-too-long` (a Reminder_Text longer than 140 UTF-16 code units), `past` (a time with the Day_Word for today that has already passed) or `too-far` (a Due_Time beyond the Reminder_Horizon).
- **Time_Printer**: The pure function in the Reminder_Module that formats a Time_Expression as text in the English form the Time_Parser accepts.
- **Due_Time_Resolver**: The pure function in the Reminder_Module that turns a Time_Expression and a receive time into a Due_Time in Pakistan_Time, or into the Parse_Error `past` or `too-far`.
- **Reminder_Text**: The part of the REMIND argument after the Time_Expression and any Filler_Words, with leading and trailing whitespace removed. A Reminder_Text is 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units long, counted as the JavaScript string length, so a character outside the Basic Multilingual Plane (such as most emoji) counts as 2.
- **Delivery_Decision**: The pure function in the Reminder_Module that takes one due Reminder, the current Gateway_State and the current time, and decides the Delivery_Outcome and the Reply_Parts to send, if any.
- **Delivery_Outcome**: The single result recorded for one Reminder that reached its Due_Time. One of these 6 values: `reminder-sent`, `reminder-stopped`, `reminder-not-allowed`, `reminder-capped`, `reminder-expired`, `reminder-failed`.
- **Reminder_Delivery_Text**: The SMS text sent at the Due_Time: a fixed lead-in in the Language of the Reminder (for example `Reminder: `) followed by the Reminder_Text.
- **List_Formatter**: The pure function in the Reminder_Module that builds the reply to LIST from the Active_Reminders of one Canonical_Number.
- **Reminder_Texts**: The fixed texts of the Reminder_Module in every Language: Reminder_Set_Text, Time_Help_Text, Past_Time_Text, Too_Far_Text, Too_Long_Text, Reminder_Limit_Text, No_Reminders_Text, Reminder_Cancelled_Text, No_Such_Reminder_Text, Cancel_Usage_Text, Reminders_Unavailable_Text and the Reminder_Delivery_Text lead-in.
- **Reminder_Set_Text**: The confirmation reply to a successful REMIND, stating the Reminder_Slot and the Due_Time in Pakistan_Time and naming the LIST and CANCEL commands.
- **Time_Help_Text**: The reply to a REMIND whose time cannot be read, giving at least 2 example REMIND messages that the Time_Parser accepts.
- **Command**: One of HELP, STOP, START, SCAM, REMIND, TRANSLATE, LIST, CANCEL, QUESTION, as defined by the Command_Router. This feature adds LIST and CANCEL to the Command set of the brain-command-router spec.
- **Reminder_Scheduler**: The Native_Module component that sets Android alarms for the next due Active_Reminder and starts reminder processing when an alarm fires.
- **Exact_Alarm_Access**: The Android permission state that lets the Necromancer_App set exact alarms. On API 26 to 30 Exact_Alarm_Access is always granted. On API 31 and later Exact_Alarm_Access is granted when `AlarmManager.canScheduleExactAlarms()` returns true for the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Route LIST and CANCEL

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want to text LIST and CANCEL 2, so that I can see and remove my reminders without an app.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Command_Router SHALL map the Keywords `list`, `fehrist` and `فہرست` to the Command LIST, and the Keywords `cancel`, `mansookh` and `منسوخ` to the Command CANCEL, using the same Normalized_Message, case-insensitive Keyword matching and trailing punctuation removal from the Keyword as the existing Alias_Table, so that `LIST`, `List?` and `cancel!` match.
2. WHEN the Keyword of a Normalized_Message is a LIST Keyword and no other token follows it, THE Command_Router SHALL return the Command LIST with an Argument of zero characters and `argumentMissing` set to false.
3. WHEN the Keyword of a Normalized_Message is a LIST Keyword and 1 or more other tokens follow it, THE Command_Router SHALL return the Command QUESTION with the full Normalized_Message as the Argument and `argumentMissing` set to false.
4. WHEN the Keyword of a Normalized_Message is a CANCEL Keyword and exactly one other token follows it that is 1 to 3 characters long and made only of ASCII digits (`0` to `9`) and Extended Arabic-Indic digits (`۰` to `۹`), THE Command_Router SHALL return the Command CANCEL with that token, unchanged and without digit conversion, as the Argument and `argumentMissing` set to false.
5. WHEN the Keyword of a Normalized_Message is a CANCEL Keyword and 2 or more other tokens follow it, or the single following token is longer than 3 characters or contains any character other than an ASCII digit or an Extended Arabic-Indic digit (including trailing punctuation such as `2.`), THE Command_Router SHALL return the Command QUESTION with the full Normalized_Message as the Argument and `argumentMissing` set to false.
6. WHEN the Keyword of a Normalized_Message is a CANCEL Keyword and no other token follows it, THE Command_Router SHALL return the Command CANCEL with an Argument of zero characters and `argumentMissing` set to true.
7. THE Command_Router SHALL keep every existing routing rule for HELP, STOP, START, SCAM, REMIND, TRANSLATE and QUESTION unchanged, including the rule that a Normalized_Message whose Keyword is `stop` or a STOP Alias returns STOP whatever follows it (for example `stop list` and `stop cancel 2` return STOP), and the rule that LIST or CANCEL Keywords appearing only after the first token do not change the routed Command.
8. WHEN the Command_Printer formats a Routed_Command with the Command LIST or CANCEL, THE Command_Printer SHALL return `LIST` or `CANCEL` alone if the Argument is empty, and `LIST` or `CANCEL` followed by a single space and the Argument if the Argument is not empty.
9. FOR ALL Routed_Commands with the Command LIST or CANCEL produced by the Command_Router, routing the output of the Command_Printer SHALL return a Routed_Command with the same `command`, `argument` and `argumentMissing` values (round-trip property; the `language` field is excluded).
10. THE Command_Router SHALL detect the Language of LIST and CANCEL messages with the same Language_Detector rules as every other Command, so that `fehrist` returns `roman-ur`, `فہرست` and `منسوخ ۲` return `ur`, and `list` and `cancel 2` return `en`.
11. THE Command_Router SHALL type the `command` field as a string literal union of exactly the 9 values HELP, STOP, START, SCAM, REMIND, TRANSLATE, LIST, CANCEL and QUESTION, and FOR ALL input strings of 0 to 1,000 characters SHALL return exactly one of these 9 Commands without throwing an error.
12. THE Brain_Test_Suite SHALL include fast-check properties that, for arbitrary letter case of each LIST and CANCEL Keyword and arbitrary trailing text of 1 to 500 characters containing at least 1 non-whitespace character, check that a LIST Keyword followed by that text routes to QUESTION, and that a CANCEL Keyword followed by that text routes to CANCEL only when the text is a single token of 1 to 3 allowed digits and to QUESTION otherwise.

### Requirement 2: Parse reminder times

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want to write the time the way I speak, in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu, so that I can set a reminder without learning a format.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline handles a REMIND message whose `argumentMissing` is false, THE Time_Parser SHALL read a Time_Expression only from the start of the argument after skipping leading whitespace, matching Latin-script words case-insensitively and accepting the ASCII digits `0` to `9` and the Extended Arabic-Indic digits `۰` to `۹` (U+06F0 to U+06F9), with both digit sets allowed in the same number and giving the same value.
2. THE Time_Parser SHALL treat one or more consecutive space, tab or newline characters as one separator between the parts of a Time_Expression, and SHALL match a Day_Word, Day_Period, Filler_Word, duration unit, `am`, `pm` or number only when it is followed by whitespace or the end of the argument, so that `kalam`, `subahdam` and `8pmx` do not match a Time_Expression part.
3. THE Time_Parser SHALL accept a 12-hour Clock_Time with an hour from 1 to 12, optional minutes from 00 to 59 written as exactly 2 digits after `:` or `.`, and `am` or `pm` written directly after the number or after one space, reading `12am` as 00:00 and `12pm` as 12:00.
4. THE Time_Parser SHALL accept a 24-hour Clock_Time of the form `H:MM` or `HH:MM` with an hour from 0 to 23 and minutes from 00 to 59, written with no `am` or `pm` after it.
5. THE Time_Parser SHALL accept a bare hour from 1 to 12, optionally followed by `:MM` or `.MM` with minutes from 00 to 59, only directly after a Day_Period, optionally separated from the Day_Period by one Filler_Word.
6. WHEN a Day_Period is followed by a bare hour, THE Time_Parser SHALL read the morning period as hours 1 to 11 before noon and 12 as 12:00, the evening period as hours 1 to 11 after noon, and the night period as hours 6 to 11 after noon, and SHALL return a Time_Expression that keeps the Day_Period.
7. WHEN a Day_Period is followed by a 12-hour Clock_Time with `am` or `pm`, or by a 24-hour Clock_Time whose hour is 0 or from 13 to 23, THE Time_Parser SHALL use the hour and minute of that Clock_Time and return a Time_Expression with no Day_Period, so that `subah 8pm` reads as 20:00.
8. WHEN a Day_Period is not followed by a Clock_Time, THE Time_Parser SHALL use 09:00 for morning, 18:00 for evening and 21:00 for night.
9. THE Time_Parser SHALL accept an optional Day_Word before a Clock_Time or a Day_Period, and an optional Day_Period between a Day_Word and a Clock_Time, as in `kal subah 9` and `tomorrow 8pm`, and SHALL return a Time_Expression with no Day_Word when none is written, leaving the choice of day to the Due_Time_Resolver.
10. THE Time_Parser SHALL accept a Relative_Duration with a whole number N from 1 to 999,999 written with 1 to 6 digits and the units minutes, hours or days, and SHALL return the duration as N × 60,000 milliseconds for minutes, N × 3,600,000 for hours and N × 86,400,000 for days, leaving durations beyond the Reminder_Horizon to the Due_Time_Resolver.
11. THE Time_Parser SHALL skip each Filler_Word that appears between the parts of a Time_Expression or directly after the Time_Expression, as in `shaam ko 6 baje` and `at 8pm`, skipping at most 1 Filler_Word at each such position.
12. IF the argument does not start with a Time_Expression, including a bare hour with no Day_Period and no `am` or `pm` (for example `REMIND 9 take medicine`), a Day_Word alone, an hour or minute outside the allowed range for its form (for example `13pm` or `20:00pm`), a Day_Period with an hour outside the range of criterion 6, or a Relative_Duration with N equal to 0 or written with more than 6 digits, THEN THE Time_Parser SHALL return the Parse_Error `no-time`.
13. WHEN the Time_Parser reads a Time_Expression, THE Time_Parser SHALL return as the Reminder_Text the rest of the argument after the Time_Expression and any trailing Filler_Word, with leading and trailing whitespace removed and with its original letters, case, script and inner whitespace unchanged.
14. IF the Time_Expression is followed by no Reminder_Text or only by whitespace, THEN THE Time_Parser SHALL return the Parse_Error `no-text`.
15. IF the Reminder_Text is longer than 140 UTF-16 code units after trimming, THEN THE Time_Parser SHALL return the Parse_Error `text-too-long`, and a Reminder_Text of exactly 140 UTF-16 code units SHALL be accepted.
16. FOR ALL strings, including empty strings, whitespace-only strings, strings with lone surrogates and strings longer than 10,000 characters, THE Time_Parser SHALL return either a Time_Expression with a Reminder_Text or a Parse_Error, and SHALL NOT throw (totality property).
17. FOR ALL strings, THE Time_Parser SHALL return the same result each time the Time_Parser is called with the same string (determinism property).

### Requirement 3: Resolve the due time in Pakistan time

**User Story:** As a Sender in Pakistan, I want "8pm" to mean 8pm my time, so that the reminder arrives when I expect.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL compute every Due_Time in Pakistan_Time from the receive time of the REMIND message, which is passed in as milliseconds since the Unix epoch. A Pakistan_Time wall-clock moment SHALL equal the same wall-clock moment in UTC minus 18,000,000 milliseconds, and the result SHALL not depend on the time zone setting of the gateway phone or on the system clock. For example, `8pm` received at 2025-01-01T15:30:00.000Z (20:30 Pakistan_Time) SHALL resolve to 2025-01-02T15:00:00.000Z.
2. WHEN a Time_Expression has a Clock_Time or Day_Period and no Day_Word, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL use that time, with seconds and milliseconds set to 0, on the current Pakistan_Time day if that moment is strictly later than the receive time. Otherwise it SHALL use the same time on the next Pakistan_Time day. A Pakistan_Time day runs from 00:00:00.000 to 23:59:59.999 Pakistan_Time, which is 19:00:00.000 UTC of the previous UTC day to 18:59:59.999 UTC.
3. WHEN a Time_Expression has the Day_Word for tomorrow, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL use the time, with seconds and milliseconds set to 0, on the Pakistan_Time day after the Pakistan_Time day of the receive time, even if the same time on the current day is still later than the receive time.
4. IF a Time_Expression has the Day_Word for today and the time on the current Pakistan_Time day, with seconds and milliseconds set to 0, is equal to or earlier than the receive time, THEN THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL return the Parse_Error `past` and SHALL NOT move the Due_Time to the next day.
5. WHEN a Time_Expression is a Relative_Duration of N units, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL set the Due_Time to the receive time plus exactly N × 60,000 milliseconds for minutes, N × 3,600,000 milliseconds for hours, or N × 86,400,000 milliseconds for days, keeping the seconds and milliseconds of the receive time.
6. WHEN a Time_Expression has a Clock_Time or a Day_Period, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL set the seconds and milliseconds of the Due_Time to 0.
7. IF the Due_Time would be more than 2,592,000,000 milliseconds (the Reminder_Horizon) after the receive time, including a Relative_Duration whose N is too large to represent as a safe integer number of milliseconds, THEN THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL return the Parse_Error `too-far`. A Due_Time exactly equal to the receive time plus the Reminder_Horizon, such as `in 30 days`, SHALL be accepted.
8. FOR ALL Time_Expressions and all receive times that are integers from 0 to 4,102,444,800,000 (2100-01-01T00:00:00.000Z), every Due_Time returned by the Due_Time_Resolver SHALL be an integer that is strictly later than the receive time and no later than the receive time plus the Reminder_Horizon (invariant property).
9. FOR ALL Time_Expressions and all receive times that are integers from 0 to 4,102,444,800,000, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL return either a Due_Time or one of the Parse_Errors `past` or `too-far`, and SHALL NOT throw (totality property).
10. FOR ALL Time_Expressions and all receive times, THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL return the same result each time it is called with the same Time_Expression and receive time (determinism property).
11. FOR ALL Time_Expressions with a Clock_Time or Day_Period and all receive times, the Due_Time returned by the Due_Time_Resolver SHALL have the hour and minute of the Time_Expression when converted to Pakistan_Time (wall-clock property).

### Requirement 4: Print time expressions

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want a printer for time expressions, so that the parser can be checked with a round trip.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Time_Printer SHALL format every valid Time_Expression as one line of lowercase English text in which: the parts appear in the order Day_Word, Day_Period, Clock_Time; the Day_Word is printed as `today` or `tomorrow`; the Day_Period is printed as `morning`, `evening` or `night`; a Clock_Time with no Day_Period is printed as a 24-hour time of the form `H:MM` or `HH:MM`; a Clock_Time with a Day_Period is printed as a bare hour from 1 to 12 of the form `H:MM` or `HH:MM` (the 24-hour hour minus 12 for hours 13 to 23, and the 24-hour hour otherwise); every hour is printed in ASCII digits without a leading zero and the minutes as exactly 2 ASCII digits; a Relative_Duration is printed as `in N minute`, `in N hour` or `in N day` when N is 1 and as `in N minutes`, `in N hours` or `in N days` otherwise, with N in ASCII digits without leading zeros; parts are separated by exactly one space; and no Filler_Word, leading space or trailing space is included.
2. FOR ALL valid Time_Expressions and all Reminder_Texts of 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units that have no leading or trailing whitespace and whose first whitespace-separated token is not a Filler_Word, a Day_Word, a Day_Period, `am`, `pm`, a Relative_Duration unit word, `baad`, `بعد` or a token starting with a digit, parsing the text made of the printed Time_Expression, one space and the Reminder_Text SHALL return a Time_Expression equal field for field to the original and a Reminder_Text equal code point for code point to the original (round-trip property).
3. FOR ALL valid Time_Expressions and all receive times from 0 to 4,102,444,800,000 milliseconds since the Unix epoch, resolving the parsed printed Time_Expression SHALL give the same Due_Time, to the millisecond, or the same Parse_Error as resolving the original Time_Expression.
4. THE Time_Printer SHALL treat as a valid Time_Expression exactly the values the Time_Parser can return: a Clock_Time with an hour from 0 to 23 and minutes from 0 to 59, a Clock_Time combined with a Day_Period only when the hour lies in the range that Requirement 2 criterion 6 allows for that Day_Period, a Day_Period alone with an optional Day_Word, and a Relative_Duration with a whole number N from 1 to 999,999.
5. FOR ALL valid Time_Expressions, THE Time_Printer SHALL return a non-empty text of at most 30 characters, SHALL return the same text each time it is called with an equal Time_Expression, and SHALL NOT throw (totality and determinism property).

### Requirement 5: Create a reminder with REMIND

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want a confirmation SMS with the time the gateway understood, so that I know my reminder is set correctly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to REMIND with `argumentMissing` false, the sender's Canonical_Number has fewer than Reminder_Limit Active_Reminders, and the Time_Parser and Due_Time_Resolver return a Due_Time and a Reminder_Text of 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL add exactly one Reminder for the sender's Canonical_Number with that Due_Time, that Reminder_Text, the Language of the Routed_Command, the receive time of the Inbound_Message as the creation time and the lowest Reminder_Slot from 1 to 5 not used by an Active_Reminder of that Canonical_Number.
2. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline adds a Reminder, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Reminder_Set_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, showing the Reminder_Slot and the Due_Time in Pakistan_Time as day of month, month, and hour and minute with the minute written as 2 digits, producing exactly 1 Reply_Part from the SMS_Splitter and containing no part of the Reminder_Text, and SHALL record the Outcome `replied`.
3. IF the Time_Parser or the Due_Time_Resolver returns `no-time`, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Time_Help_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder.
4. IF the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to REMIND with `argumentMissing` true, or the Time_Parser returns `no-text`, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Usage_Hint_Text for REMIND in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder.
5. IF the Time_Parser returns `text-too-long` for a Reminder_Text longer than 140 UTF-16 code units, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Too_Long_Text, which states the 140-character limit, in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder.
6. IF the Due_Time_Resolver returns `past`, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Past_Time_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder.
7. IF the Due_Time_Resolver returns `too-far` for a Due_Time more than Reminder_Horizon after the receive time of the Inbound_Message, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Too_Far_Text, which states the 30-day limit, in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder.
8. IF the sender's Canonical_Number already has Reminder_Limit Active_Reminders when a REMIND message with `argumentMissing` false arrives, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Reminder_Limit_Text, which states the limit of 5 and names LIST and CANCEL, in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, record the Outcome `replied` and add no Reminder, whether or not the Time_Parser and Due_Time_Resolver would return a Parse_Error for that message.
9. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline handles REMIND, LIST or CANCEL, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL make no call to the Answer_Service, so that these Commands produce the same Reply_Text and Reminder changes whether or not a Gemini_Api_Key is stored.
10. THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL apply the Automated_Sender, Own_Number, Stop_List, Allowlist_Mode, Rate_Limiter and Daily_Send_Cap checks of necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 7 criterion 3 to REMIND, LIST and CANCEL messages before any reminder handling, and SHALL add or remove no Reminder for a message that one of those checks ends.
11. IF the Daily_Send_Cap check of necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 5 criterion 8 blocks the Reply_Parts of a Reminder_Set_Text, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL record the Outcome `send-capped`, add no Reminder and leave the Reminder_Slots of every existing Active_Reminder of that Canonical_Number unchanged.
12. THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL add a Reminder only for the Canonical_Number of the sender of the REMIND message, and SHALL add no Reminder for any phone number that appears inside the REMIND argument.
13. FOR ALL sequences of REMIND messages from one Canonical_Number, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL keep at most Reminder_Limit Active_Reminders for that Canonical_Number, with pairwise distinct Reminder_Slots from 1 to 5 (invariant property).
14. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline handles a REMIND message with `argumentMissing` false, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL apply the Reminder_Limit check of criterion 8 before calling the Time_Parser, and SHALL produce exactly one Reply_Text and record exactly one Outcome for that message.

### Requirement 6: List reminders

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want LIST to show my reminders with their numbers, so that I know which one to cancel.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to LIST and the sender's Canonical_Number has 1 to 5 Active_Reminders, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the reply built by the List_Formatter in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and SHALL record the Outcome `replied`.
2. THE List_Formatter SHALL list every Active_Reminder of the Canonical_Number exactly once. The Active_Reminders SHALL be in ascending order of Due_Time, and Active_Reminders with equal Due_Times SHALL be in ascending order of Reminder_Slot. For each Active_Reminder the List_Formatter SHALL show its Reminder_Slot, its Due_Time in Pakistan_Time in the same day-and-time form used by the Reminder_Set_Text, and a preview of its Reminder_Text. The preview SHALL be the whole Reminder_Text when it is 20 code points or shorter, and otherwise its first 20 code points, unchanged and untranslated.
3. IF the full list would produce more than 3 Reply_Parts from the SMS_Splitter, THEN THE List_Formatter SHALL apply one shared maximum preview length to every Active_Reminder. That length SHALL be the largest whole number from 0 to 19 code points for which the reply produces at most 3 Reply_Parts. At length 0 the reply SHALL contain no preview, and the Reminder_Slot and Due_Time of every Active_Reminder SHALL still be present.
4. FOR ALL sets of 1 to 5 Active_Reminders with Reminder_Texts of 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units in any Language, THE List_Formatter SHALL return a reply that produces 1 to 3 Reply_Parts from the SMS_Splitter and contains the Reminder_Slot and Due_Time of every Active_Reminder (invariant property).
5. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to LIST and the sender's Canonical_Number has 0 Active_Reminders, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the No_Reminders_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and SHALL record the Outcome `replied`.
6. THE List_Formatter SHALL include only Active_Reminders whose Canonical_Number equals the sender's Canonical_Number. A reply to LIST SHALL contain no Reminder_Text, Reminder_Slot or Due_Time of a Reminder that belongs to any other Canonical_Number.
7. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline handles a LIST message, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL leave the Reminder_Store unchanged: no Reminder is added or removed, and no Reminder_Slot or Due_Time changes.
8. IF the Reminder_Store cannot be read while the Gateway_Pipeline handles a LIST message, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Reminders_Unavailable_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and SHALL leave the Reminder_Store unchanged.

### Requirement 7: Cancel one reminder

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want CANCEL 2 to remove reminder 2, so that I can fix a mistake.

WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to CANCEL with an argument n and the sender's Canonical_Number has an Active_Reminder in Reminder_Slot n, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL remove that Active_Reminder together with its Reminder_Text from the Reminder_Store, use the Reminder_Cancelled_Text with slot n in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and record the Outcome `replied`.

WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline handles a CANCEL argument, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL read the argument as a base-10 whole number n, treating each Extended Arabic-Indic digit (`۰` to `۹`) as the ASCII digit of the same value and ignoring leading zeros, so that `2`, `02`, `002` and `۲` all select Reminder_Slot 2.

IF the sender's Canonical_Number has no Active_Reminder in Reminder_Slot n, including when n is 0, when n is from 6 to 999, and when the Active_Reminder in slot n was already delivered, expired or cancelled, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL leave every Reminder unchanged, use the No_Such_Reminder_Text, which names LIST, in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and record the Outcome `replied`.
4. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline routes an accepted Inbound_Message to CANCEL with `argumentMissing` true, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL leave every Reminder unchanged, use the Cancel_Usage_Text, which shows the form `CANCEL` followed by a number and names LIST, in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, and record the Outcome `replied`.
5. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline removes an Active_Reminder under criterion 1, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL leave every other Reminder unchanged, including the Reminder_Slots, Due_Times and Reminder_Texts of the sender's other Active_Reminders and every Reminder of every other Canonical_Number.
6. WHEN a Reminder_Slot of a Canonical_Number becomes free through CANCEL, delivery, expiry or STOP, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL treat that Reminder_Slot as unused, so that the next successful REMIND from the same Canonical_Number receives that Reminder_Slot if it is the lowest unused Reminder_Slot from 1 to 5 under Requirement 5 criterion 1.
7. IF the Daily_Send_Cap check of necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 5 criterion 8 blocks the Reply_Parts of a Reminder_Cancelled_Text, THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL still remove the Active_Reminder of criterion 1, send no Reply_Parts, and record the Outcome `send-capped`.
8. FOR ALL Reminder_Stores, all Canonical_Numbers and all CANCEL arguments n from 0 to 999, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL remove at most 1 Reminder when handling CANCEL n, and any removed Reminder SHALL belong to the sender's Canonical_Number and have Reminder_Slot n (invariant property).

### Requirement 8: STOP cancels reminders

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want STOP to cancel my reminders too, so that I get no more texts after I opt out.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline records the Outcome `stopped` for a Canonical_Number, including STOP while paused and STOP applied from a Pending_Stop_Record, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL remove every Reminder of that Canonical_Number, including its persisted Reminder_Text, from the Reminder_Store in the same state change that adds the Canonical_Number to the Stop_List, so that no state produced by the Reminder_Store_Model has the Canonical_Number on the Stop_List while it still has an Active_Reminder.

FOR ALL Reminder_Stores and all Canonical_Numbers, after the Gateway_Pipeline handles a STOP from that Canonical_Number, the Reminder_Store SHALL contain no Reminder of that Canonical_Number and SHALL contain exactly the same Reminders of every other Canonical_Number as before (property: STOP removes all reminders for that number).
4. WHILE a Canonical_Number is on the Stop_List, WHEN a Reminder of that Canonical_Number reaches its Due_Time, THE Delivery_Decision SHALL apply the Stop_List check before the Allowlist_Mode, Late_Window and Daily_Send_Cap checks, SHALL produce no Reply_Parts, SHALL count nothing toward the Daily_Send_Cap and SHALL record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-stopped`, so that the Necromancer_App removes that Reminder under Requirement 10 criterion 8 and STOP wins even when the Reminder removal of criterion 1 has not yet been persisted.
5. WHEN a Canonical_Number sends START after STOP, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL restore no Reminder that criterion 1 removed, so that a LIST from that Canonical_Number sent right after START gets the No_Reminders_Text and the next REMIND gets Reminder_Slot 1.
6. IF persisting the Reminder removal of criterion 1 fails, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL keep the removal in the in-memory Reminder_Store used for delivery and SHALL retry the write together with the Stop_List write under necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 3 criterion 12.
7. FOR ALL sequences of Inbound_Messages and Reminder deliveries for one Canonical_Number, THE Gateway_Pipeline and THE Delivery_Decision together SHALL produce no Reply_Parts for any Reminder delivery that is decided after a STOP from that Canonical_Number and before the next START, whatever the Due_Time of that Reminder, including a Reminder whose Due_Time fell before the STOP and that is still inside the Late_Window (property: STOP always wins for reminders).
8. WHEN the Necromancer_App loads the Reminder_Store at start, including a start by the Boot_Receiver, THE Necromancer_App SHALL remove every Reminder whose Canonical_Number is on the Stop_List before the Reminder_Scheduler sets any alarm.
9. WHEN the Gateway_Pipeline removes Reminders under criterion 1, THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL set its alarm for the earliest Due_Time among the remaining Active_Reminders of all Canonical_Numbers, or SHALL set no alarm when no Active_Reminder remains.

### Requirement 9: Reminder texts

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want every reminder reply short and in my language, so that I can read it on a button phone.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Reminder_Module SHALL define every Reminder_Texts item for each of the 3 Languages `en`, `ur` and `roman-ur`, with no item empty and no item containing a newline character.
2. THE Reminder_Module SHALL write every `en` and `roman-ur` Reminder_Texts item using only characters of the GSM-7 basic character set, with no GSM-7 extension-table characters, and SHALL write every `ur` Reminder_Texts item in Urdu script, where the only non-Urdu-script characters allowed are the digits 0 to 9, spaces, punctuation and the command keywords of criterion 6.
3. FOR ALL Languages, every Reminder_Texts item other than the Time_Help_Text and the Reminder_Delivery_Text lead-in SHALL produce exactly 1 Reply_Part from the SMS_Splitter. The Reminder_Set_Text, Reminder_Cancelled_Text and No_Such_Reminder_Text SHALL be checked with every Reminder_Slot from 1 to 5, and the Reminder_Set_Text SHALL also be checked with Due_Times covering every day of month from 1 to 31, every month from 1 to 12, and the times 00:00, 12:59 and 23:59 in Pakistan_Time.
4. FOR ALL Languages, the Time_Help_Text SHALL contain at least 2 example REMIND messages and SHALL produce 1 or 2 Reply_Parts from the SMS_Splitter.
5. FOR ALL example REMIND messages in the Time_Help_Text of every Language, THE Command_Router SHALL route the example to the Command REMIND with `argumentMissing` false, THE Time_Parser SHALL return a Time_Expression and a Reminder_Text of 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units with no Parse_Error, and THE Due_Time_Resolver SHALL return a Due_Time, not `past` or `too-far`, for every receive time.
6. FOR ALL Languages, every REMIND, LIST or CANCEL keyword that appears in a Reminder_Texts item of a Language SHALL be a keyword that the Command_Router routes to that same Command when an Inbound_Message in that Language starts with it, and every `CANCEL n` example in the Cancel_Usage_Text SHALL route to the Command CANCEL with a whole number from 1 to 5.
7. FOR ALL Reminder_Texts the Time_Parser accepts (1 to 140 UTF-16 code units), including Reminder_Texts made only of characters outside the GSM-7 basic character set, GSM-7 extension characters or characters outside the Basic Multilingual Plane, and for all Languages, the Reminder_Delivery_Text SHALL start with the lead-in of that Language followed by the unchanged Reminder_Text, SHALL produce 1 to 3 Reply_Parts from the SMS_Splitter, and joining the Reply_Parts after removing their part prefixes SHALL give back the Reminder_Delivery_Text exactly.

10. WHEN the Routed_Command has the Command TRANSLATE and `argumentMissing` false, THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Not_Available_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text, unchanged from necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 6.

### Requirement 10: Decide reminder delivery

**User Story:** As an Operator, I want reminders to follow the same safety rules as replies, so that reminders never break STOP, the allowlist or the daily cap.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Necromancer_App asks the Delivery_Decision about an Active_Reminder whose Due_Time is equal to or earlier than the current time, THE Delivery_Decision SHALL run its checks in this fixed order: Stop_List (Requirement 8 criterion 4), Allowlist_Mode (criterion 2), Late_Window (criterion 3), Daily_Send_Cap (criterion 4). The first check that applies SHALL decide the Delivery_Outcome, and no later check SHALL run for that Reminder.
2. WHERE Allowlist_Mode is on, IF the Canonical_Number of the Reminder is not on the Allowlist, or the Allowlist cannot be read, THEN THE Delivery_Decision SHALL produce no Reply_Parts and record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-not-allowed`.
3. IF the current time is more than the Late_Window of 3,600,000 milliseconds after the Due_Time (current time > Due_Time + 3,600,000), THEN THE Delivery_Decision SHALL produce no Reply_Parts and record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-expired`. A current time of exactly Due_Time + 3,600,000 SHALL NOT expire the Reminder.
4. IF the number of SMS parts already handed to the Sms_Sender during the current Daily_Send_Cap calendar day (phone local time zone), counting replies to Inbound_Messages and earlier Reminder_Delivery_Texts, plus the number of Reply_Parts of this Reminder_Delivery_Text is greater than the Daily_Send_Cap, THEN THE Delivery_Decision SHALL produce no Reply_Parts and record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-capped`. A total exactly equal to the Daily_Send_Cap SHALL NOT cap the Reminder.
5. WHEN none of Requirement 8 criterion 4 or criteria 2 to 4 applies, THE Delivery_Decision SHALL record no Delivery_Outcome yet and SHALL produce the Reply_Parts of the Reminder_Delivery_Text, split by the SMS_Splitter, in the Language of the Reminder, addressed only to the Canonical_Number of the Reminder.
6. WHEN the Sms_Sender reports that every Reply_Part of a Reminder_Delivery_Text was sent, THE Necromancer_App SHALL record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-sent` and add the number of Reply_Parts to the sent count of the current Daily_Send_Cap calendar day.
7. IF the Sms_Sender reports a Reply_Part as failed after the single retry of necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 8, or reports `refused-stopped` for any Reply_Part, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-failed` or `reminder-stopped` respectively, hand no further Reply_Part of that Reminder to the Sms_Sender, and add only the number of Reply_Parts the Sms_Sender reported as sent (0 or more) to the sent count of the current Daily_Send_Cap calendar day.
8. WHEN a Delivery_Outcome is recorded for a Reminder, THE Necromancer_App SHALL remove that Reminder, including its Reminder_Text, from the Reminder_Store and free its Reminder_Slot, so that each Reminder produces at most 1 Delivery_Outcome and its Reminder_Delivery_Text is handed to the Sms_Sender at most once.
9. THE Delivery_Decision SHALL leave the Rate_Limiter records unchanged for every Delivery_Outcome.
10. FOR ALL due Reminders, all Gateway_States and all current times, THE Delivery_Decision SHALL return either exactly one of the Delivery_Outcomes `reminder-stopped`, `reminder-not-allowed`, `reminder-expired` or `reminder-capped` with 0 Reply_Parts, or no Delivery_Outcome with 1 or more Reply_Parts. Each Reminder that reaches criterion 6 or 7 SHALL end with exactly one of the 6 Delivery_Outcomes (invariant property).
11. WHEN one alarm, boot, service start or resume passes 2 or more due Active_Reminders to the Delivery_Decision, THE Necromancer_App SHALL ask the Delivery_Decision about them one at a time, in ascending order of Due_Time and then of Reminder_Id. Each decision SHALL use a sent count that includes the Reply_Parts handed to the Sms_Sender for the Reminders decided before it in the same run.
12. IF the Stop_List cannot be read when a Reminder is due, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL hand no Reply_Parts of that Reminder to the Sms_Sender, record no Delivery_Outcome, keep the Reminder in the Reminder_Store, and ask the Delivery_Decision about it again after the next successful Stop_List read, with the Late_Window of criterion 3 measured from the original Due_Time.
13. IF the Necromancer_App asks the Delivery_Decision about a Reminder whose Due_Time is later than the current time, THEN THE Delivery_Decision SHALL produce no Reply_Parts and no Delivery_Outcome, and THE Necromancer_App SHALL keep the Reminder in the Reminder_Store unchanged.
14. IF persisting the Reminder removal of criterion 8 fails, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL keep the removal in the in-memory Reminder_Store used for delivery, hand no Reply_Part of that Reminder to the Sms_Sender again, and retry the write up to 3 times at 1-second intervals. IF all 3 retries fail, THEN THE Main_Screen SHALL show an error message telling the Operator that reminders could not be saved.
15. IF the Necromancer_App restarts or the phone reboots after at least 1 Reply_Part of a Reminder was handed to the Sms_Sender and before its Delivery_Outcome was recorded, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL hand no Reply_Part of that Reminder to the Sms_Sender again, record the Delivery_Outcome `reminder-failed`, and remove the Reminder under criterion 8.

### Requirement 11: Schedule and send reminders on the device

**User Story:** As a Sender, I want my reminder to arrive on time even if the gateway phone restarted, so that I can rely on it for medicine.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE Exact_Alarm_Access is granted, THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL set an exact alarm that is allowed while the phone is idle for the earliest Due_Time among the Active_Reminders.
2. WHILE Exact_Alarm_Access is not granted, THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL set an inexact alarm that is allowed while the phone is idle for the earliest Due_Time among the Active_Reminders, and THE Gateway_Service SHALL keep running with no crash and no error dialog shown to the Operator.
3. WHEN an Active_Reminder is added, delivered, cancelled, expired or removed by STOP, THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL, within 5 seconds, set the alarm for the new earliest Due_Time among the remaining Active_Reminders, or cancel the pending alarm if no Active_Reminder remains.
4. WHEN an alarm of the Reminder_Scheduler fires, THE Necromancer_App SHALL load the Reminder_Store, pass every Active_Reminder whose Due_Time is not later than the current time to the Delivery_Decision one at a time, in ascending order of Due_Time and then of Reminder_Id under Requirement 10 criterion 11, and then set the alarm for the next earliest Due_Time.
5. WHILE Exact_Alarm_Access is granted, the phone is on, the gateway is not paused and the Sms_Sender is not busy with another multipart SMS, THE Necromancer_App SHALL hand the Reply_Parts of a Reminder_Delivery_Text to the Sms_Sender no earlier than the Due_Time and no later than 60 seconds after the Due_Time.
6. WHEN the phone finishes booting, the Necromancer_App package is replaced, the Gateway_Service starts, the phone's clock or time zone is changed, the Operator resumes the gateway, or Exact_Alarm_Access changes from not granted to granted, THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL, within 10 seconds of the event, set the alarm again from the persisted Active_Reminders, and THE Necromancer_App SHALL pass every Active_Reminder whose Due_Time has passed to the Delivery_Decision.
7. WHILE the Operator has paused the gateway, THE Necromancer_App SHALL send no Reminder_Delivery_Text, SHALL pass no Active_Reminder to the Delivery_Decision, and SHALL keep every Active_Reminder unchanged in the Reminder_Store.
8. THE Necromancer_App SHALL declare the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission, and THE Necromancer_App SHALL NOT declare the USE_EXACT_ALARM permission.
9. WHILE Exact_Alarm_Access is not granted on API 31 or later, THE Main_Screen SHALL show a message stating that reminders can arrive late, together with a button that opens the Android exact alarm settings page for the Necromancer_App, and THE Main_Screen SHALL check Exact_Alarm_Access again each time it comes to the foreground and hide the message within 1 second once Exact_Alarm_Access is granted.
10. THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL keep at most 1 pending alarm for reminders at any time.
11. THE Necromancer_App SHALL send every Reminder_Delivery_Text through the existing Sms_Sender, so that the Stop_List check before every send attempt, the Send_Timeout and the single retry of necromancer-sms-gateway Requirement 8 apply to reminders.
12. THE Necromancer_App SHALL contain no function of its own that parses a Time_Expression, resolves a Due_Time, assigns a Reminder_Slot, formats a reminder reply or decides a Delivery_Outcome, and SHALL import that logic from `@lazarus/brain`.
13. IF setting an exact alarm fails because Exact_Alarm_Access was withdrawn, THEN THE Reminder_Scheduler SHALL set an inexact alarm for the same Due_Time instead, keep every Active_Reminder unchanged, and keep the Gateway_Service running with no crash.
14. THE Necromancer_App SHALL pass each Active_Reminder to the Delivery_Decision at most once and SHALL hand the Reply_Parts of each Reminder_Delivery_Text to the Sms_Sender at most once, including when an alarm and a trigger of criterion 6 occur at the same time and when the Necromancer_App restarts after the hand-off.
15. IF the Reminder_Store cannot be read when an alarm fires or a trigger of criterion 6 occurs, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL send no Reminder_Delivery_Text, keep every stored Reminder unchanged, record an Activity_Entry stating that reminder processing failed, and set the alarm to fire again 60 seconds later.

### Requirement 12: Store reminders in SQLite

**User Story:** As an Operator, I want reminders kept on the phone across restarts, so that no reminder is lost when the phone reboots.

6. WHEN the Necromancer_App restarts or the phone reboots, THE Necromancer_App SHALL load exactly the Active_Reminders that were persisted before the restart, with the same count and an equal value in every field, including Active_Reminders whose Due_Time passed while the Necromancer_App was not running.

IF the Reminder_Store cannot be read (the `reminder` table cannot be opened or queried, the upgrade under criterion 4 failed, or a loaded row has a Reminder_Slot outside 1 to 5 or a Reminder_Text outside 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units), THEN THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL use the Reminders_Unavailable_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command as the Reply_Text for REMIND, LIST and CANCEL messages, add or remove no Reminder, keep handling STOP under Requirement 8 and every other Command as before, and THE Main_Screen SHALL show a message stating that reminders could not be loaded until the Reminder_Store is read without error.
8. FOR ALL Reminders with Reminder_Texts of 1 to 140 UTF-16 code units in English, Urdu script or Roman Urdu, writing a Reminder to the Reminder_Store and reading the Reminder_Store back SHALL return a Reminder equal in every field (round-trip property, checked in the Brain_Test_Suite on the state wire format).
9. THE Necromancer_App SHALL exclude the `reminder` table from Android auto-backup and device-to-device transfer through the existing `database` exclusion.
10. WHEN a Reminder stops being an Active_Reminder because it was delivered, cancelled, expired or removed by STOP, THE Necromancer_App SHALL delete its row from the `reminder` table in the transaction of criterion 5, so that its Reminder_Text no longer appears in any row of `lazarus.db`.
11. IF persisting a Reminder change under criterion 5 fails, THEN THE Necromancer_App SHALL roll back that transaction, leave every row of the `reminder` table as it was before the Inbound_Message or delivery, and THE Gateway_Pipeline SHALL send the Reminders_Unavailable_Text in the Language of the Routed_Command in place of the Reminder_Set_Text or Reminder_Cancelled_Text, while STOP keeps being handled under Requirement 8.

### Requirement 13: Reminder privacy and dashboard

THE Necromancer_App SHALL show and write no Reminder_Text, no Reminder_Delivery_Text and no LIST reply text on the Main_Screen, the Settings_Screen, the persistent notification, the Activity_Log (including the Activity_Entries of REMIND, LIST and CANCEL Inbound_Messages), Android logcat, JavaScript console output or any error message shown or logged by the Necromancer_App.
